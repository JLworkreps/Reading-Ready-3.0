1. Account Purpose. Reading-Ready accounts are provided exclusively for authorized DepEd personnel (Reading Coordinators and Teachers) to record, monitor, and act on Phil-IRI reading assessment data for learners under their care.

2. Data Accuracy. You agree to encode assessment scores truthfully and promptly correct any errors using the system's edit tools once identified.

3. Learner Data Privacy. Learner names, scores, and reading levels accessed through this system are confidential educational records. You agree not to share this data outside authorized school or division use, consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

4. Account Approval. New Reading Coordinator and Teacher accounts require review and approval by a system admin or Reading Coordinator before first login. Submitting this form does not guarantee approval.

5. Acceptable Use. You agree not to attempt unauthorized access to other schools' data, share your login credentials, or use the system for any purpose outside its intended educational use.
