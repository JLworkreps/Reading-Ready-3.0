How a score becomes a classification

The same mark-up teachers already make

While a learner reads a passage aloud, a teacher marks it up exactly like this — circling omitted words, noting substitutions, tracking self-corrections. Reading-Ready doesn't replace that process; it takes the resulting counts (words read correctly, comprehension questions answered) that a teacher already tallies, and classifies the reading level and speed instantly — no separate spreadsheet, no manual percentage math after the fact.

What is Phil-IRI, and what does Reading-Ready do with it?

The Phil-IRI is DepEd's standard tool for checking how well a learner reads at their grade level. A teacher gives a written screening test, then listens to the learner read a passage aloud and answer comprehension questions — three times a year (Pre-test, Mid-year, Post-test). Reading-Ready takes those same scores, already familiar to every Filipino reading teacher, and turns the paperwork into something you can actually act on:

FrustrationNeeds intensive support
InstructionalGuided reading needed
IndependentReading on their own

Every learner lands on this line automatically, computed straight from the raw scores a teacher already writes down — no manual tallying, no separate spreadsheet.

One place for Phil-IRI data

Pre-test, mid-year, and post-test scores are encoded once and classified instantly into Independent, Instructional, or Frustration — per learner, per term.

See problems before report cards

Dashboards, charts, and an early-warning risk indicator flag a learner who's likely to fall behind, weeks before the next official test confirms it.

Targeted help, not guesswork

Every struggling learner's profile shows exactly which skill they're weak in — word recognition, literal, inferential, or critical comprehension — with matched reading materials ready to assign.

Built for real school workflows

Separate views for the System Admin, Reading Coordinator, Teacher, and the learner themselves — everyone sees exactly what they need, nothing more.
